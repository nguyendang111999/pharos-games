About the studio

A small, steady light in your day

Pharos Games is an independent studio making casual and puzzle games for mobile devices. We're named after the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria, which stood on the island of Pharos. Our goal is just as simple: be a friendly little beacon that brings a few relaxing, rewarding minutes to your day.

Easy to pick up

Simple rules and clear goals. No manual required, just jump in and play.

Satisfying to solve

Thoughtful puzzles built around that moment when everything clicks into place.

Made for mobile

Short sessions that fit a commute, a coffee break, or a quiet moment on the couch.
