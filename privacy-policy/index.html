Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Pharos Games ("Pharos Games", "we", "us" or "our") makes casual and puzzle games for mobile devices (the "Games"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play our Games or visit our website (the "Website"), how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to all Games published by Pharos Games.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Games.

1. Summary

The short version:

  • You do not need to create an account to play our Games.
  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.
  • We and our partners collect limited device and usage data, including your device's advertising ID, to run the Games, understand how they are used, fix crashes, and show ads.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money.
  • Our Games are not directed to children under 13.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see Section 13).

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information collected automatically

When you use our Games, we and our third-party service providers (see Section 6) automatically collect:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, screen settings, app version, and randomly generated app or installation identifiers.
  • Advertising identifier: your Android Advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings.
  • IP address and approximate location: your IP address and the country, region or city it indicates. We do not collect precise (GPS) location.
  • Gameplay and usage data: levels played, scores and progress, features used, session length, in-game events, and how you interact with ads and in-app purchase offers.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports, error logs and performance data.

2.2 Information you provide to us

If you contact us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and anything else you choose to tell us.

2.3 Purchases

If a Game offers in-app purchases, they are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation of the purchase (such as the product, order ID and purchase token) but we never receive or store your payment card or billing details.

2.4 Information we do not collect

We do not require an account, and our Games do not collect your precise location, contacts, text messages, photos or media files, camera or microphone recordings, or health information. Your settings and game progress are stored on your device.

2.5 This Website

This Website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses and other technical information for security and operational purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email us, we use your email address only to reply.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • provide, operate and maintain the Games, including saving your settings;
  • understand how players use the Games, balance gameplay, and develop new features and games;
  • detect, diagnose and fix bugs and crashes;
  • show ads, which help us keep our Games free, and measure how those ads perform;
  • process and verify purchases and prevent fraud, cheating and abuse;
  • respond to your questions and requests;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK & Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we process your information on these legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide the Games you have asked to use.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the Games secure, prevent fraud, fix crashes, understand usage and improve our Games, balanced against your rights and interests.
  • Consent: for personalized advertising and for storing or accessing identifiers on your device where the law requires it. You can withdraw consent at any time (see Section 5).
  • Legal obligation: where we must process information to comply with the law.

5. Advertising

Our Games are free to play and may show ads provided by third-party advertising partners. These partners may use your advertising ID and similar technologies to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and measure performance. Depending on your location and settings, ads may be personalized based on your interests, or may be non-personalized (based on context such as the game you are playing).

Your choices:

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can review or change your choice at any time through the privacy options in the Game's settings menu, where available.
  • On Android, you can opt out of ads personalization or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads; the exact path varies by device). Learn more at adssettings.google.com.
  • Opting out does not remove ads. You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

7. How we share information

We share information only in these situations:

  • Service providers: the third-party services listed in Section 6, which process information on our behalf or for their own purposes as described in their policies.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, court order or a valid request from authorities, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our players, ourselves or others.
  • Business changes: if Pharos Games is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • With your consent: in any other case where you ask or agree to it.

We do not sell your personal information in exchange for money. However, sharing advertising identifiers and usage data with advertising partners may be considered a "sale", "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some laws, such as those of California and other US states. See Section 12 for your choices.

8. Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. Analytics and diagnostics data is kept for a limited period determined by our settings and our partners' retention policies. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period afterwards. Game data stored on your device stays there until you clear it or uninstall the Game. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, and we choose reputable service providers. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International transfers

We and our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States, where data protection laws may differ. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as adequacy decisions, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses, put in place by us or our service providers.

11. Children's privacy

Our Games are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13 (or under the higher minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will delete it.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights:

  • Everyone: to ask what information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct or delete it.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your information, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California and other US states: to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to delete or correct it; to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.
  • Elsewhere: many other countries (for example Brazil, Canada and Australia) provide similar rights, and we honor them where they apply.

To exercise a right, email us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We will respond within 30 days, or sooner if the law requires. We may need to verify your request. Because we do not run player accounts, we may ask you for a device identifier, such as your advertising ID, so that we can find the data that relates to your device.

To opt out of personalized advertising, see Section 5. Authorized agents may submit requests on your behalf with proof of authorization.

13. Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete the data we hold about you at any time:

  1. Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the subject line "Data deletion request".
  2. Tell us which Game you play and, if you can, your advertising ID, so we can find your data.
  3. We will delete or anonymize the data we hold and ask our service providers to do the same, normally within 30 days. We will confirm by email when this is done.

You can also remove data stored on your device by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the Game, and you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings. We may keep limited information where we are legally required to, for example records of purchases.

14. Third-party links

The Games and Website may contain links to third-party websites, apps or ads. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies before giving them any information.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date. For significant changes we will also take additional steps where appropriate, such as notifying you in the Game. Please review this page periodically.
